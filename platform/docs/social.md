# @stg/social contract

Phase 8a: friends, chat (rooms, groups, direct messages, word filter, moderation), private chat quota and friend gifts. Depends on `@stg/base`, `@stg/players` (tags, mutes, suspensions, reports), `@stg/economy` (metas, wallets, prizes, payments), `@stg/gamification` (levels, events), `@stg/items` (inventory, product rules) and `@stg/publication` (inbox notices). Ported from nakama-farsi `docs/roadmap.md` rows 3, 17, 22, 23 and 26 and `docs/base/spec.md` («Friends and player reports», «Private chat quota») and `docs/economy/spec.md` («Friend gifts»), adapted to tables, reducers, procedures and views.

## Roles

`operator` reads every list, edits the chat word list, deletes and restores messages and closes groups. `admin` changes every setting and rule, rooms, giftable flags, quota grants, reverses gifts and moves the test clock. Every admin change writes `base_audit`. Player calls need a logged-in, active, not suspended account (`requireAccount`).

## Hooks into other packages

- `@stg/players`: `fileReport(ctx, me, {targetId, titleKey, note, matchId, contentId, gameKey}, system)` is the body of `report_player`, exported so chat reports land in `players_report` (rate limits, duplicate window, review and decisions stay there). `system = true` skips the reporter limits (filter flags, reporter id 0).
- `@stg/economy`, new generic hooks:
  - `grantOverrides: (tx, payment, product) => rewardsJson | undefined`, run in `grantPayment` after `settleChecks`; a hook that answers takes over the grant (friend gifts grant to the recipient or hold the prize).
  - `payeeHooks: (ctx, payment) => accountId | undefined` and `payeeOf(ctx, payment)`: who receives a payment's prize (default the payer). Read-only, safe in views.
- `@stg/items`: `settleChecks` (one-time and owned contents) check the payee, and purchase counts (`boughtOnce`, `deliveredCount`, `my_purchases`) skip payments whose payee is someone else, so a gift is not the sender's purchase.
- `metaUseChecks`, `currencyUseChecks`, `itemUseChecks`: metas, currencies and items named in social settings cannot be deleted (`IN_USE`).
- `recordEvent`: `Gift:Sent`, `Gift:Received` (seeded system event types).
- `sendNotification`: inbox (or inbox + push) notices in the seeded category `social`.
- `logActivity`: `friend/requested|accepted|removed|blocked`, `prize/gift_sent|gift_received|gift_returned|gift_reversed`.

## Time and test clock

Friend days and gift days are Tehran days (UTC+3:30). The quota day starts at `resetMinute` in the zone `utcOffsetMinutes` (Iran has no DST). Views have no clock: they return stored state (`expiresAt` fields) and every "now" decision happens in reducers, procedures and the 30 s `social_tick`. `admin_set_social_clock_offset(seconds)` (admin, audited, ±3650 days) moves this package's clock only (stored in `social_settings.clockOffsetSec`); `admin_social_run()` runs a sweep at once (invite expiry, pending gift expiry, paying gift sync, rate rows). Keep the offset 0 in production.

## Friends

Tables (private): `social_edge` (one row per direction, key `a|b`, `state` = `friend`, `sent`, `received`, `blocked`, `expiresAt` for invites), `social_friend_day` (new requests per player and Tehran day), `social_rejection` (who declined whom, for the cooldown).

Settings `social_friend_settings` (id 0): `dailyRequests` (20; 0 = no limit, ≤1000), `maxFriends` (0, ≤10000), `expiryDays` (0 = never, ≤365), `rejectCooldownHours` (0, ≤8760), `matchPolicy` (`priority` | `generous`), `requestsBonusMeta`, `friendsBonusMeta` (economy metas; the whole, non-negative value adds to a non-zero limit), `overLimit` (`refuse` | `trim`). `admin_friend_settings` view, `admin_save_friend_settings` (`INVALID_RULE:<field>`, `INVALID_SETTING:<field>`, `META_NOT_FOUND`).

Tag rules `social_friend_rule`: `name`, `tagIds` (player tags, 1–20), `priority` (−1000…1000), optional `dailyRequests`, `maxFriends`, `rejectCooldownHours` (none = keep the default, 0 = unlimited / no wait), `isActive`. `priority`: the highest-priority matching rule overrides field by field; `generous`: per field the most generous value (0 beats any number, then the largest; the shortest wait). `admin_friend_rules` view, `admin_save_friend_rule` (`TAGS_REQUIRED`, `TAG_KIND_MISMATCH`, `TAG_NOT_FOUND`, `TOO_MANY_TAGS`, `LIMIT_REQUIRED`, `INVALID_PRIORITY`, `INVALID_RULE`), `admin_delete_friend_rule`.

Player calls:

- `friend_request(targetIds)` (1–100, `NO_USERS`, `FRIEND_TOO_MANY_USERS`, `CANNOT_TARGET_SELF`, `USER_NOT_FOUND`) → `{sent, accepted, skipped, remainingToday}`. A target that already invited the caller is accepted; friends, already invited and blocked (either side) are skipped silently, so a blocked player learns nothing. Then: `FRIEND_LIMIT_REACHED` (caller full), `FRIEND_REQUEST_COOLDOWN` (the target declined the caller within the cooldown), `FRIEND_DAILY_LIMIT` (refuse: more new invites than left; trim: the first ones go). Accepting is free. Requests are serialized transactions, so parallel calls never pass the limit. Reaching out to someone clears the cooldown they would have against you.
- `friend_accept(accountId)` (`REQUEST_NOT_FOUND`, `REQUEST_EXPIRED`, `FRIEND_LIMIT_REACHED`, `FRIEND_TARGET_LIMIT_REACHED`), `friend_decline` (records a rejection), `friend_cancel`, `friend_remove` (`NOT_FRIENDS`), `friend_block` (ends any friendship or invite), `friend_unblock` (`NOT_BLOCKED`).
- `friend_rules()` → `{dailyRequests, sentToday, remainingToday?, resetsAt, maxFriends, friends, friendSlotsLeft?, expiryDays, rejectCooldownHours, base, bonus, tier?, overLimit, matchPolicy}` (effective values).
- Views (caller only): `my_friends` (`FriendEntry`), `my_friend_requests_in`, `my_friend_requests_out` (`FriendRequestEntry` with `expiresAt`), `my_blocked`.

Admin: `admin_player_friends(accountId)` → edges and the player's rules; `admin_remove_friendship(accountId, otherId)`.

## Chat

Tables (private): `social_channel` (`kind` = `room`, `group`, `direct`; rooms `room:<key>` with translated `title`/`description`; groups `name` and `ownerId`; direct `dm:<a>:<b>` with `ownerId` = who started it), `social_member`, `social_message` (`text` as others see it, `original` when the filter changed it, `status` = `visible`, `deleted` by the sender, `removed` by staff, `flagged`, `hits`), `social_rate`.

Settings `social_settings` (id 0): `chatEnabled`, `messageMaxLength` (500), `messagesPerMinute` (20), `dmPolicy` (`anyone` | `friends` | `none`), `groupsEnabled`, `groupMaxMembers` (50), `groupsPerPlayer` (10), `filterEnabled`, `filterReportTitle` (`abuse`), `historyPageMax` (100), `liveMessages` (30 newest per channel in the live view). `admin_chat_settings`, `admin_save_chat_settings`.

Player calls:

- `chat_send(channelId, peerId, text, replyToId)` → `{ok, error, messageId, channelId, text, masked, flagged, quota?}`. `channelId = 0` with `peerId` sends a direct message and opens the channel. Order: `CHAT_OFF`, `CHAT_MUTED` (players restriction), `TEXT_REQUIRED`, `MESSAGE_TOO_LONG`, `CHANNEL_NOT_FOUND`, `NOT_MEMBER`, `CHANNEL_REQUIRED`, `CANNOT_TARGET_SELF`, `USER_NOT_FOUND`, `BLOCKED` (either side), `DIRECT_OFF`, `NOT_FRIENDS` (`dmPolicy = friends`), `REPLY_NOT_FOUND`, `TOO_MANY_MESSAGES`, `CHAT_BLOCKED` (filter), then the quota. A quota refusal is not thrown: `ok = false`, `error` = `DM_QUOTA_EXCEEDED` | `DM_CONVERSATION_LIMIT` and `quota` (below), so the refusal is counted.
- `chat_join_room`, `chat_leave` (a group owner hands the group to the oldest member; an empty group closes), `chat_mark_read(channelId, messageId)` (0 = newest), `chat_delete_message` (own), `chat_history(channelId, beforeId, limit)` → `{rows, hasMore}` newest first (members only; senders the caller blocked are hidden).
- Groups: `chat_create_group(name, memberIds)` → `{channelId}` (members must be friends: `NOT_FRIENDS`, `BLOCKED`; `GROUPS_OFF`, `GROUP_FULL`, `TOO_MANY_GROUPS`, `NAME_BLOCKED`, names are masked), `chat_group_add`, `chat_group_remove`, `chat_group_rename` (owner only, `NOT_GROUP_OWNER`, `ALREADY_MEMBER`).
- `chat_report(messageId, titleKey, note ≤500)` → `{reportId, duplicate}`: a `players_report` with `contentId = chat:<id>`, `matchId = channel:<id>`, `gameKey = chat`; once per reporter and message; `MESSAGE_NOT_FOUND` (also when the caller is not in the channel), `CANNOT_REPORT_OWN`, plus the players report errors.
- Views: `chat_rooms` (public, active rooms), `my_channels` (`ChatChannelEntry` with `peerId`, `role`, `unread`), `my_chat_live` (`ChatLiveMessage`, the newest `liveMessages` of each of the caller's channels; deleted messages have empty text).

### Word filter

`social_word`: `word`, `normalized` (unique), `action` (`mask`, `block`, `flag`), `match` (`word`, `contains`), `isActive`, `note`. Ten words are seeded once (`wordsSeeded`). Normalisation: compatibility decomposition, Arabic diacritics, tatweel, ZWNJ/ZWJ, bidi marks and soft hyphen removed, `ي ى ئ` → `ی`, `ك` → `ک`, `ة ە ھ ۀ` → `ه`, `أ إ آ ٱ` → `ا`, Persian and Arabic digits to Latin, lower case, common Cyrillic and Greek look-alikes to Latin, leet next to Latin letters (`0 1 3 4 5 7 @ $ !`), repeated letters collapsed. `word` matches a token (or two adjacent tokens, or a run of single letters) with an optional Persian or English suffix; multi-word entries match consecutive tokens; `contains` matches anywhere with separators removed. Masking replaces matched letters with `*`, keeps spaces and punctuation and drops invisible marks inside the match. `block` refuses (`CHAT_BLOCKED`), `flag` passes and files a filter report (reporter 0, title `filterReportTitle`).

Admin: `admin_chat_words` view, `admin_save_chat_word` (`INVALID_WORD`, `INVALID_MOD_ACTION`, `INVALID_MATCH`, `ALREADY_EXISTS` by normalised form), `admin_delete_chat_word`, `admin_test_chat_filter(text)` → `{text, normalized, changed, blocked, flagged, hits}`.

### Moderation

`admin_rooms` view, `admin_save_room(id, key, title, description, isActive, sort)` (`INVALID_KEY`, `ALREADY_EXISTS`), `admin_delete_room` (with members and messages). `admin_social_messages(channelId, senderId, query, flagged, status, page, pageSize)` (query also searches the original text), `admin_social_message(id)` → message, five messages around it and its reports, `admin_delete_chat_message(id, note)` (`ALREADY_REMOVED`), `admin_restore_chat_message` (`NOT_REMOVED`), `admin_chat_groups(query, page, pageSize)`, `admin_close_group`. Report review and decisions (warn, mute, suspend) use the players report pages.

## Private chat quota

Off by default: nothing is counted or stored until `enabled`. Tables: `social_quota_settings` (id 0), `social_quota_rule`, `social_quota_day` (one row per player and quota day: `messages`, `conversations`, `friendMessages`, `friendConversations`, `packUsed`, `freeSent`, `unlimitedSent`, `total`, `blocked`, `notified`), `social_quota_target` (first and last message per target `u:<peer>`, `g:<id>`, `r:<id>`, and `replyOwed`), `social_quota_lot` (packs: `amount`, `remaining`, `source` `meta` | `admin`, `expiresAt`).

Settings (defaults): `enabled` (false), `countMode` (`messages` | `conversations` | `both`), `dailyMessages` (20), `dailyConversations` (5), `conversationScope` (`day` | `ever`), `resetMinute` (0), `utcOffsetMinutes` (210, −720…840, `INVALID_TIMEZONE`), `countDirect` (true), `countGroups`, `countRooms` (false), `replyFree` (`off` | `started` | `reply`), `friendPolicy` (`same` | `separate` | `free`), `friendDailyMessages` (50), `friendDailyConversations` (10), `packMeta` (whole units of the meta move into a lot on the next send or status call; the meta keeps the fraction), `packExpiryDays` (0 = never, from the purchase), `order` (`free_first` | `packs_first`), `packProducts` (product keys shown on refusal; empty = active products whose prize adds to `packMeta`), `bonusMeta` (added to the free daily quota), `matchPolicy`, `unlimitedItems` (owning any = unlimited), `unlimitedMeta` + `unlimitedMetaMode` (`positive` | `until` a Unix time), `message` (translated refusal text with `{reset}`, `{limit}`; built-in fa/en), `notice` (`none` | `inbox`, first refusal of the day). `SAME_META`, `META_NOT_FOUND`, `ITEM_NOT_FOUND`, `PRODUCT_NOT_FOUND`, `INVALID_SETTING:<field>`.

Gate order inside `chat_send` (after moderation, so a refused or blocked message never costs quota): unlimited (rule, item, meta) → free to friends → free reply (`started`: the peer opened the chat; `reply`: one free reply after each of their messages) → cost. `messages`: each message costs a free unit or a pack unit; `conversations`: only the first message of a new chat costs a conversation unit; `both`: messages cost units and new chats also hit a hard cap (`DM_CONVERSATION_LIMIT`, packs do not help). With `separate`, messages to accepted friends use the friend counts. Packs are spent oldest expiry first. Refusal `quota` = `{code, unit, limit, used, packsLeft, resetsAt, friend, rule?, message, products[{id, key, amount}]}`.

Calls: `chat_quota()` → `{enabled, countMode, unlimited, unlimitedBy, rule?, freeLeft, packsLeft, packsExpireAt, order, day, resetsAt, messages, conversations, friends?, friendPolicy, replyFree, bonus, today{sent, fromPacks, free, unlimited, blocked}, products}`. Admin: `admin_quota_settings` view, `admin_save_quota_settings`, `admin_quota_rules` view, `admin_save_quota_rule` (`unlimited` or a limit, `LIMIT_REQUIRED`), `admin_delete_quota_rule`, `admin_player_quota(accountId)` (status, 20 lots, 14 days), `admin_quota_grant(accountId, amount, expiresInDays (−1 = settings, 0 = never), note)`, `admin_quota_reset(accountId)` (today's free counters), `admin_quota_revoke_lot(id)`, `admin_quota_report(days)` (totals, daily series, top refused players, packs from metas and from the panel).

## Friend gifts

Tables: `social_gift_settings` (id 0), `social_gift_rule`, `social_giftable` (`kind:key` flags for `item`, `item_type`, `product`; an item's flag beats its type's flag, both beat the default), `social_gift` (the gift and its state), `social_gift_request` (request ids), `social_gift_day` (sender, recipient and pair counts per Tehran day).

Settings (defaults): `enabled` (true), `recipients` (`friends` | `any`), `minFriendDays`, `minMutualFriends`, `minSenderAccountDays`, `minRecipientAccountDays`, `minSenderLevel` + `levelTableId` (0), `inventoryEnabled` (true), `itemsDefault` (true), `equipped` (`block` | `unequip`), `upgradeLevel` (`keep` | `reset`), `regift` (`block` | `allow`), `itemRequirements` (`check` | `ignore`), `currencyEnabled` (false), `currencies` (`[{currencyKey, min, max}]`, max 0 = no limit), `shopEnabled` (true), `productsDefault` (true), `methods` (`in_app`, `gateway`, `store`), `discountCodes` (false), `perSenderDaily` (5), `perRecipientDaily` (10), `perPairDaily` (2), `matchPolicy`, `sendBonusMeta`, `receiveBonusMeta`, `delivery` (`instant` | `accept`), `expiryHours` (72), `onExpiry` (`return` | `accept`), `coinReturn` (`refund` | `sender` | `none`), `moneyReturn` (`sender` | `refund_due` | `none`), `senderCancel` (true), `messageEnabled` (true), `messageMaxLength` (120), `messageFilter` (`rules` | `block` | `off`, the chat word list), `notify` (`in_app` | `push_in_app` | `none`), `notifySender` (true). Tag rules `social_gift_rule`: `perSenderDaily`, `perRecipientDaily`, `perPairDaily` (none = keep, 0 = no limit), `canSend`; the sender's rules give the sender and pair limits and `canSend`, the recipient's rules the receive limit.

Status: `sending`, `paying` (shop gift waiting for its payment), `pending` (waiting for the recipient), `delivered`, `declined`, `expired`, `cancelled`, `reversed`, `failed`. Outcome: `delivered`, `returned`, `refunded`, `sender_got`, `refund_due`, `kept`. A gift counts against the day it was sent unless it never left the sender (`failed`, `cancelled`).

Calls:

- `gift_options(friendId)` → `{friendId, friendName, isFriend, canSend, reason, limits{sender, recipient, pair: {used, limit, remaining?, bonus}, day, rule?}, delivery, expiryHours, messageEnabled, messageMaxLength, discountCodes, items[{itemKey, quantity, level, equipped, recipientOwns, giftable, reason}], currencies[{currencyKey, min, max, balance}], products[{productId, key, priceToman, coinCurrency, coinAmount, methods, giftable, reason}]}`.
- `gift_send(friendId, itemKey | currencyKey+amount | productId+method(+giftCode), quantity, message, requestId)` → `{giftId, status, replayed, purchase?}`. Inventory items and currency move inside one transaction (instant: straight to the recipient with source `gift`; accept: held in the gift). A shop gift creates the gift (`paying`) and starts a normal economy purchase paid by the sender with context `friend_gift:<id>`: coins settle at once, gateways redirect, stores answer `status = store` and finish with `purchase_submit`; when paid, the prize goes to the recipient (`economy_user_prize.sourceType = friend_gift`) or waits for acceptance. A payment that fails, expires or is refused fails the gift. The same `requestId` (`[A-Za-z0-9_.:-]{1,64}`) answers the first gift with `replayed = true`; other details → `REQUEST_ID_REUSED`.
- `gift_accept(giftId)` (recipient; delivery is checked again), `gift_decline` (recipient), `gift_cancel` (sender, `GIFT_CANCEL_OFF`). Declined, cancelled and expired gifts give items and currency back; products follow `coinReturn` / `moneyReturn`.
- Views: `my_gifts_received` (no price or payment; `paying`, `sending` and `failed` gifts are hidden), `my_gifts_sent` (with `method` and `paymentId`).

Errors: `FRIEND_GIFTS_OFF`, `INVENTORY_GIFTS_OFF`, `CURRENCY_GIFTS_OFF`, `SHOP_GIFTS_OFF`, `GIFT_SELF`, `GIFT_SOURCE_REQUIRED`, `RECIPIENT_NOT_FOUND`, `GIFT_BLOCKED` (either side, also with `any`), `NOT_FRIENDS`, `FRIENDSHIP_TOO_NEW`, `NOT_ENOUGH_MUTUAL_FRIENDS`, `SENDER_ACCOUNT_TOO_NEW`, `RECIPIENT_ACCOUNT_TOO_NEW`, `SENDER_LEVEL_TOO_LOW`, `GIFT_SENDING_BLOCKED`, `GIFT_SENDER_DAILY_LIMIT`, `GIFT_RECIPIENT_DAILY_LIMIT`, `GIFT_PAIR_DAILY_LIMIT`, `ITEM_NOT_FOUND`, `ITEM_NOT_OWNED`, `STARTER_ITEM`, `ITEM_NOT_GIFTABLE`, `ITEM_EQUIPPED`, `REGIFT_BLOCKED`, `RECIPIENT_OWNS_ITEM`, `STACK_FULL`, `GIFT_ALREADY_PENDING`, `RECIPIENT_REQUIRES_ITEM`, `RECIPIENT_LEVEL_TOO_LOW`, `RECIPIENT_LEVEL_TOO_HIGH`, `CURRENCY_NOT_GIFTABLE`, `INVALID_AMOUNT`, `INSUFFICIENT_BALANCE`, `PRODUCT_NOT_FOUND`, `PRODUCT_NOT_GIFTABLE`, `METHOD_NOT_ALLOWED`, `RECIPIENT_OWNS_PRODUCT`, `MESSAGE_TOO_LONG`, `MESSAGE_BLOCKED`, `GIFT_MESSAGES_OFF`, `INVALID_REQUEST_ID`, `REQUEST_ID_REUSED`, `GIFT_NOT_FOUND`, `GIFT_NOT_PENDING`, `GIFT_EXPIRED`, `GIFT_CANCEL_OFF`, plus the payment errors of `purchase_start`.

Admin: `admin_gift_settings` view, `admin_save_gift_settings` (`INVALID_SETTING:<field>`, `LEVEL_TABLE_NOT_FOUND`, `CURRENCY_NOT_FOUND`, `DUPLICATE_CURRENCY`, `INVALID_AMOUNT`, `META_NOT_FOUND`), `admin_gift_rules` view, `admin_save_gift_rule`, `admin_delete_gift_rule`, `admin_giftables` view, `admin_set_giftable(kind, key, giftable?)` (none = back to the default), `admin_friend_gifts(status, kind, userId, flagged, page, pageSize)`, `admin_friend_gift(id)` (row plus the audit trail of `gift:<id>`), `admin_reverse_friend_gift(id, returnTo, note)`: a pending gift is returned; a delivered one is taken back from the recipient (`ITEM_GONE`, `INSUFFICIENT_BALANCE`; product rewards are undone: currency, added metas, items) and goes to `returnTo` = `sender`, `refund` (coin purchases only, `INVALID_POLICY`) or `none`; `GIFT_NOT_REVERSIBLE` otherwise. `admin_friend_gift_report(days)`: counts by status and kind, senders, recipients, money and coins spent, currency moved, top senders and recipients, daily series.

## Not in this phase

Panel pages; friend suggestions and friend notices; chat stickers and edits; voice; live socket notices beyond the views (clients subscribe to `my_*` views instead).
